title: Creating my own website
date: 2026-08-13
description: Why did I create my own website?

I’ve always had the idea of creating my own website in the back of my mind ever since I started programming in high school.

HTML and CSS were two of the first languages I learned, after all. I’ve always liked designing: choosing colours, working with composition, 
making things look pretty. I really liked CSS because it felt different from drawing by hand. You have to think in a different way. With pen and paper you can 
just put a photo or part of a drawing wherever you want it, but with computers you have to think about layers, positions, angles and percentages. I found it 
fun to just play around with the different numbers.

I never seriously considered it until a friend of mine told me she wanted to make a website for her CV, and that really made me think. The job market within IT 
isn’t looking the greatest right now. The people who get interviews and jobs are often the ones with the most interesting resumes, and I thought that creating 
a webpage as a resume could give me a different edge that people outside of programming don’t have. It also communicates things that traditional resumes can’t. 
An example is colours and design. Most resumes are just Word documents, but if I give people a website, they can also see my overall vibe and get to know me in 
a way a single sheet of paper can’t. I like deep red and emerald green, cute things, and I like to climb and play games. Yes, you can read about this in my resume, 
but it’s different when you can see it.

From then on, I kept thinking about creating a website for myself, but I never did. I kept putting it off because I didn’t really know what the website should be. 
I looked around the internet for inspiration, but I couldn’t justify spending all that time creating an entire page just for a resume.

Then I met someone who had already created his own page, and what I saw was really cool. He didn’t just use it as a resume; he also had a blog, socials and projects 
on it. He used it as a kind of personal hub where everything was connected. Compared to what I’d seen before, just a resume and some projects, this felt much more 
personal and alive.

That’s when I finally decided: I want one too.
